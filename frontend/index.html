[ .WHY TWO PRICES ]

The stock feed is the real-world NVDA/USD quote, bought from x402 sellers and paid through Circle Gateway nanopayments, then published onchain by the agent. The onchain price is NVDAc's market price, read from the real trading pools through The Graph. No external oracle provider is used — when the two drift beyond the deviation band, the engine stops taking new risk.
