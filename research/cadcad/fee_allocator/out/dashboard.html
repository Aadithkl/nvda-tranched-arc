The 1/6 rule is the point where the whole 0.30 pool collects as many fees as the 0.05 pool: $97.96M, or 12.9× its current volume. Our own fee equality is much earlier because our 0.30 share is 12.8× our 0.05 share: 100% in 0.30 equalizes at ≈1.01× current (1.30% of 0.05), and a 28% sleeve is fee-positive even at ≈0.89× current. The live rule replaces the hurdle with a 0.30 risk gate; the 28% sleeve can be delayed by thin volume, volatility, guard, depeg or the share cap. Total-fee parity is not the switch rule.

4. What the model assumes

The 0.15 pool has no native tape. It uses the 0.30 hourly fee shape at half the fee rate and the selected volume. The 0.30 volume multiples hold liquidity fixed; real volume growth would attract liquidity and dilute the fee rate. Sleeves are passive: no re-centering, no skew. The IL drag is common across pools and is shown as a separate overlay.
