Net carry = book fees + arm USDG + NVDA yield − borrow − gas. The residual “LP mark/timing” is the LP value versus the HODL basket, including the value of being in the arm during the down move. 8. Assumptions

Book A is a V3 0.05% proxy; no V4 USDG/NVDA 0.05% pool exists in the downloaded set. Book B is the real V4 0.30% pool. Arm: 3% USDG lend, 3% NVDA total yield APR after borrowing cost, 3% borrow, $15k borrow cap, 20% relative arm caps. Gas $0.02/action. No MEV/slippage/price impact. Both the 45d and last-20d windows are down regimes; the bootstrap preserves the same regime. ETH/NVDA is a separate quote-asset comparison; ETH/USD is not in the tape.
