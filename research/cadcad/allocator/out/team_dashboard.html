  • Fills are at the tape price; no queue, MEV or price impact.

  • Depeg is exogenous and off by default; calibration is future work.

A 2h/15k/75 · carry waterfall (45d)

Better fees, more IL, more net

The 75% guard routes more fees to the reserve; the 15k volume floor keeps the book out of thin hours; the 2h keeper interval reduces churn.

Book fees are 99%+ of net carry. In the focus run, fees are $280.40 against $277.89 of net carry (100.9% of carry). Treat the arm as optionality, not the carry engine.

07

Controls

Guard share, keeper interval and LTV. The controls change the shape of the P&L, not just its level.

Sensitivity bars are against the risk-only 1h/$10k/50 base; the focus run is 2h/$15k/75.

Guard share · 0 / 25 / 50 / 75%

75 = 75% share, not 75 bps

Higher guard share keeps more fees in the reserve and reduces book risk. It is a fee/IL trade-off: the 45d window rewards 75%, the 20d window punishes it.

Keeper interval · 1 / 2 / 4 / 8h

Rebalance cadence, not guard cadence

2h is the best in-sample interval in both windows, but the 20d spread (+$30 vs −$130) says the ranking is fragile. The 200 bps guard is evaluated on 5-minute moves and is separate from this interval.

75% guard is a regime bet. Over 45 days it is the best of the four guard settings (+$273.69); over 20 days it is the worst (−$127.14). Do not read the 75% share as 75 bps, and do not confuse the 5-minute guard threshold with the keeper interval.

What would change our mind

  • A fresh tape. This is one 45-day down regime; a different regime can reorder the guard and interval settings.
  • A larger bootstrap. Twenty paths can rank medians; it cannot certify a p05. Require a 100+ path p05 before sizing.
  • Book B depth. The 0.30% pool is thin; a depth and price-impact model could erase the Book B advantage at size.
  • Arm rate verification. The 3% USDG / 3% NVDA / 3% borrow scenarios are flat assumptions, not a full Morpho rate history.
  • Guard EV calibration. The 200 bps pull proxy needs a realized-loss study before the 75% guard share is promoted.

Tape metadata: 36-minute risk window; 200 bps guard threshold; 1,080 hours. The 5-minute moves are a proxy, not a live auction.

09

Team decision

What the team should take away, and what to do next.

Five things to agree on

  • The book is the business. Fees are 99%+ of net carry. Optimize the book before optimizing the arm.
  • Risk-only A at a $15k floor is positive in both windows (+$314.17 over 45d, +$28.26 over 20d); the 1h/$10k baseline is +$191.04 / −$105.62. The floor is doing real work. It also has a shallower drawdown than Always A (run risk_only_a_vol15k).
  • A 2h/15k/75 is the best in-sample run (+$431.36 / +$46.95), but its bootstrap p05 is worse than Always A. C/D are higher in-sample but have worse p05; do not deploy them without a tail hedge or a smaller skew. Do not promote the focus run on the point estimate alone.
  • Always A is the risk-adjusted benchmark. It is the configuration every new idea has to beat on mean and p05, not just on median.
  • Book B is thin. Cap it at $250k–$500k and do not let a 45-day fee number justify uncapped depth. Next is a fresh tape plus paper trading, not live capital.

Ask: approve a fresh-tape and paper-trading phase. Do not move live capital on a 20-path, in-sample dashboard.

Future change not in this tape

The target design — 45-minute risk window, no volume floor, 24-hour A/B split and a low-fee exit — is not in this tape. The current run is a 36-minute risk window, $15k volume floor and 200 bps 5-minute pull proxy. Re-run the allocator after the tape update; until then, treat every 45m / no-floor result as unmeasured. The 45d and 20d labels are window lengths, not the 45-minute risk setting.

Caveats

Read these before quoting any number.

45-day down regimeNVDA fell over both windows (HODL −3.2% / −5.0%). A rally would reorder the book/arm and guard results.
Book A is a V3 0.05% proxyNo V4 USDG/NVDA 0.05% pool was available. Book A fees use the V3 pool as a fee-equivalent proxy; the fee match is +0.19%.
Guard pull proxyGuard pulls are 200 bps on 5-minute moves, not a live auction or a Chainlink depeg. Guard 75 is a 75% fee share.
Arm rates are scenarios3% USDG, 3% NVDA and 3% borrow are flat assumptions. No full Morpho rate history and no liquidity crunch.
3% NVDA is a Spiral total-yield assumptionIt is APR after borrowing cost, not a native Morpho collateral yield. The model grosses it up by LTV × borrow APR and subtracts borrow; the net NVDA contribution is 3% of collateral.
Gas is $0.02 per action210 actions cost about $4.20. A congested chain or a larger action count would change the small-alpha runs.
No MEV or price impactFills are at the tape price. Book B depth and swap routing are not simulated; both would reduce net.
Depeg is offThe depeg proxy is exogenous and not calibrated. A real USDG depeg would hit the vault and the books at the same time.
