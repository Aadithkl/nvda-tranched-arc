Lowering the 3h volume floor from $50k to $25k did not create a winner: the 5% caps still never enter, and the 15–25% entries that do happen lose $418–$3,424.

6. What would have to be true

The 10× case is not a forecast. It holds the 0.30 pool's liquidity fixed while multiplying its volume 10×, which lifts its fee APR to roughly 190%. In reality that volume would attract more liquidity and dilute the fee APR. The honest reading is: the 0.30 pool needs roughly an order of magnitude more volume at today's depth, and a share cap around 15–25%, before it pays for the concentration risk. At the actual tape, it does not.
